# 🎯 Quick Demo Instructions

## Test the Complete Flow in 2 Minutes! ⚡

### Step 1: Open Website (5 seconds)
```
Double-click: index.html
```

### Step 2: Add Food to Cart (30 seconds)
- Click "Add to Cart" on any 3-4 items
- Example: Classic Burger + Margherita Pizza + Chocolate Cake

### Step 3: View Cart (10 seconds)
- Click cart icon (top right corner)
- See your selected items
- Adjust quantities if needed
- Click "Checkout"

### Step 4: Fill Details (30 seconds)
**Customer Information:**
- Name: John Doe
- Email: john@example.com
- Phone: +91 9876543210
- Address: 123, Main Street, Mumbai

**Payment Method:** Select "Cash on Delivery" (fastest for demo)

### Step 5: Place Order (5 seconds)
- Click "Place Order" button
- Wait for success message

### Step 6: Download Receipt (10 seconds)
- Success modal appears
- Click green "📄 Download Receipt (PDF)" button
- PDF downloads automatically!

### Step 7: View Your Receipt
- Open Downloads folder
- Find: `FoodHub_Receipt_ORD[timestamp].pdf`
- Open and see your professional receipt! ✨
